Our services

Three services, carefully delivered.

Whether you need a qualified interpreter in the room, a precise written translation, or accurate transcription, we match the work to the right linguist for the setting.

— i —

Interpretation

In-person, telephonic, consecutive, and simultaneous interpretation for healthcare, legal, government, education, business, and conference settings.

— ii —

Translation & Transcription

Written translation and accurate transcription for legal documents, consent forms, publications, research studies, and multilingual communications.

— iii —

Voiceover & Subtitles

Native-voice voiceover, dubbing, and accurate multilingual subtitling for training videos, broadcast, educational media, and corporate communications.

About the firm

A family firm with a long memory.

Founded in 1993 by Vietnamese immigrants in the Bay Area, Lan Do & Associates was built to help organizations bridge communication across languages and cultures with care, accuracy, and professionalism.

✦

For more than three decades we have continuously served healthcare, legal, government, education, community organizations and businesses with interpretation, translation, and transcription services grounded in cultural understanding, confidentiality, and reliability.

✦

Our work remains practical and service-focused: matching clients with qualified linguists, protecting sensitive communications, and helping people understand and be understood in important settings.

Professional standards

A standard, carefully kept.

Three principles guide how we hire, how we work, and how we protect our clients' communications.

i

Qualified linguists

Experienced interpreters and translators support healthcare, legal, government, education, and business settings. Many hold federal, state, and/or professional certifications.

ii

Accuracy & confidentiality

Our linguists emphasize professionalism, cultural competence, accuracy, and confidentiality when handling sensitive communications.

iii

Translation expertise

Translation and transcription support includes legal documents, consent forms, research studies, publications, websites, business materials, political campaigns, and official records.
