About Lan Do & Associates, LLC

Lan Do & Associates, LLC was established in 1993 and has maintained steady growth. Our talent pool consists of more than five hundred highly qualified linguists, painstakingly recruited from a wide variety of backgrounds within the Nation's array of cultures, and subjected to intensive testing prior to being accepted as sub-contractors.

We translate pamphlets, protocol materials, brochures, and consent forms, providing finished products formatted to exact specifications and camera-ready for print. Our philosophy is to bridge the communications gap between monolingual speakers and service providers by offering quality services at competitive rates.

We serve clients throughout California, the United States, and internationally.
