Contract Interpreter Opportunities

Paid independent contractor interpreting assignments for bilingual and multilingual speakers.

About Lan Do & Associates

Lan Do & Associates is a California-based language services company that has worked with interpreters since 1993. We provide interpretation services for healthcare, government, nonprofit, and professional clients, including phone, video, and select in-person assignments.

The opportunity

We are expanding our interpreter network and are looking to connect with qualified bilingual and multilingual speakers across many languages. Assignments may vary by language, location, client need, and interpreter qualifications.

What we look for

  • Fluency in English and at least one other language
  • Strong communication and listening skills
  • Professionalism, reliability, and confidentiality
  • Ability to follow client instructions and interpreting protocols
  • Prior interpreting experience, training, or certification preferred, but not always required

Languages of interest

All languages are welcome. Languages of interest include, but are not limited to:

Vietnamese Cantonese Mandarin Spanish Tagalog Burmese Karen Chin Dari Pashto Farsi Punjabi Arabic Russian Ukrainian Nepali Hindi Gujarati Telugu Tamil Korean Japanese

If your language isn't listed, we still encourage you to apply.

Independent contractor status

— Please note —

These are independent contractor opportunities, not employee positions. Assignment availability is not guaranteed and may depend on language demand, client needs, location, credentials, and experience.
