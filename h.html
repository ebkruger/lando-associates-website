About the firm

A family-owned firm with a long memory.

Founded in 1993 by Vietnamese immigrants in the Bay Area — built on care, accuracy, and the everyday work of being understood.

Helping people understand and be understood, in the moments that matter most.
Founded 1993 Alameda, California · Woman-owned, minority-owned, family-run.

Founded in 1993 by Vietnamese immigrants in the Bay Area, Lan Do & Associates was built to help organizations bridge communication across languages and cultures with care, accuracy, and professionalism.

For more than three decades, we have supported healthcare, legal, government, education, and business communities with interpretation, translation, and transcription services grounded in cultural understanding, confidentiality, and reliability.

Our work remains practical and service-focused: matching clients with qualified linguists, protecting sensitive communications, and helping people understand and be understood in important settings.

Professional standards

A standard, carefully kept.

Three principles guide how we hire, how we work, and how we protect our clients' communications.

1

Qualified linguists

Experienced interpreters and translators support healthcare, legal, government, education, and business settings. Many hold federal, state, or professional certifications.

2

Accuracy & confidentiality

Our linguists emphasize professionalism, cultural competence, accuracy, confidentiality, and discretion when handling sensitive communications.

3

Translation expertise

Translation and transcription support includes legal documents, consent forms, research studies, publications, websites, business materials, and official records.
